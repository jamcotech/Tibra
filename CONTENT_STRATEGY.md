## 1. Editorial Mission & Brand Voice

TIBRA NATURALS produces authentic, botanical-first topical wellness formulations designed for active living. Our editorial content serves to educate, build consumer trust, demystify plant extraction science, and encourage healthy daily movement rituals.

### Core Editorial Principles
1. **Evidence-Aware & Transparent:** Discuss traditional uses of herbs (Turmeric, Ginger, Boswellia, Eucalyptus, Wintergreen) responsibly without asserting unapproved clinical cure claims.
2. **Accessible & Practical:** Explain dosages, application techniques, and massage routines in plain, accessible language with Roman Urdu conversational relevance.
3. **Internal Linking Integrity:** Every educational article must contextually reference related ingredient breakdowns and direct users toward the authentic product catalog.
4. **Anti-Spam Quality Bar:** Reject mass-produced low-quality AI copy; each piece provides distinct, actionable, high-utility insights.

## 3. On-Page Content Guidelines

- **Primary Heading (H1):** Exactly one descriptive, keyword-aligned H1 per article.
- **Subheadings (H2, H3):** Scannable hierarchy addressing specific user intent questions.
- **Key Takeaways Box:** A bulleted executive summary at the beginning of each guide.
- **Contextual Call-to-Action:** Mid-article and closing product feature linking directly to `/products/pain-relief-oil/`.
- **E-E-A-T Footprint:** Clear author attribution ("TIBRA NATURALS Botanical Advisory Team"), verified publication dates, and strict safety disclaimers.
